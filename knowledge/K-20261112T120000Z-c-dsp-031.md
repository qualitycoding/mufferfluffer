statement: "BBD delay relation: total delay = N / (2*f_clock) for N stages (Raffel and Smith Sec 2.1, DAFX-2, citing an external reference); worked examples there: 4096 stages at about 6826.7 Hz gives 300 ms (input bandlimit about 3413 Hz) and 1024 stages at 51.2 kHz gives 10 ms (bandlimit 25.6 kHz). Holters and Parker state the signal is delayed by N/2 clock periods (Sec 2, Eq. 1, DAFX-2). Coolaudio datasheet delay ranges fit the same law (V3205: 4096 stages, 10-100 kHz, 20.48-204.8 ms; V3208: 2048 stages, 10-100 kHz, 10.24-102.4 ms; V3207: 1024 stages, 10-200 kHz, 2.56-51.2 ms - the formula itself is not printed in those datasheets; fit by the agent)."
